# Markets

## What it is for

Every trade and every holding carries a market: Crypto, Forex, Stocks, Futures,
Commodities, Indices, Bonds or Options. The market is a label you choose, and it
is the axis the Journal and Portfolio can be filtered by.

## How it works

- **New entries are prefilled.** The form opens on your default market from
  Settings → Preferences, so the common case needs no extra tap.
- **Filters in Overview and Stats.** Both tabs scope their table, statistics
  and charts to one market, or to all of them. The filter always starts on
  **All markets** and is not remembered between visits.
- **Unspecified is a real choice.** Records made before markets existed are
  marked Unspecified, and it is the default for a fresh install. It means "no
  market recorded", not "unknown asset".
- **A fixed list, on purpose.** The nine markets are built in and translated
  like the rest of the interface.

## Why it works this way

- **One axis, two tabs.** A book that mixes crypto, forex and stocks is common,
  but per-market analysis is what makes the mix useful. A single shared label
  keeps the Journal and the Portfolio comparable.
- **Unspecified beats a guess.** Backfilling old data with a plausible market
  would corrupt every per-market statistic after it. Unspecified keeps the data
  honest, and you can reclassify a record by editing it.
- **The default market is not a filter.** It pre-fills new entries only. Views
  stay on All markets so a forgotten preference never hides your records.
- **A fixed list keeps stats clean.** Free-text markets invite "crypto",
  "Crypto" and "CRYPTO" as three separate buckets.
